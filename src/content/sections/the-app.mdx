Two of the event kinds in that transcript are not rows in a log. They are **the
loop stopping for a human**, and they are the whole reason a window exists
instead of a terminal.

A **question** renders as a form — the question, its options with their
descriptions, and a free-text field that goes through verbatim — while the
profile it came from stays on screen beside it. In a terminal that profile
scrolled away four screens ago, which is the entire argument.

A **durable write** renders as its fields laid out rather than dumped: the
step's keys, its `grain`, its `expect`, its `rationale`. If the step overrides a
blocking flag, that override is a banner above everything else and it does not
collapse. A spec once shipped a table with 3.85% too much revenue because an
override appeared as fifteen characters in the middle of a single-line dict in a
terminal prompt. The banner is the second chance at that.
