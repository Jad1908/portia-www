**This section has no lede and that is deliberate.** It used to open *not a chat log* and then
explain that the conversation is where the work happens — a defence against a doubt no visitor had
raised, which is the exact move this file's own notes say the page stopped making. The title asserts
what you walk away with, the three claims say what it is good for, and the explaining is gone.

Nothing in this section names a file format. The first version of it did, and
what that bought was precision about the wrong thing: the reader does not care
what the artifact is written in, only whether it survives being handed to
somebody else.
