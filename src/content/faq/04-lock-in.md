order: 4
question: "What am I left with if I stop using it?"
---

Plain files in your own repo. The decisions are YAML; the pipeline is one
committed `.sql` per table, dbt-shaped, with a generated header. Delete portia
and the SQL still runs, the diffs still read, and the reasoning is still in the
specs beside them.

That is not generosity, it is the premise. The format is the thing worth
standardising and the tooling is the thing worth building; a durable artifact
you cannot take with you is not durable, it is a hostage.
